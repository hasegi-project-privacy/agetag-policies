Article 4 (Provision of the Service)

  1. The Company provides the following content through the Game:
    • A two-team alley game based on the Korean neighborhood game "Nai-meokgi" (Age Tag): two teams (you and AI kids) each guard their own utility pole. Every kid carries an age over their head; an older kid who tags a younger opponent, or anyone who touches the other team's pole, grows older. Equal ages are settled by rock-paper-scissors, teammates who hold hands add their ages together, and a team can match ages and call "Let's go at thirty!" for a group rock-paper-scissors showdown. The win condition is chosen at the start of every round: "Age race" (the first team whose every member reaches the chosen age cap wins) or "Timed" (the team with the higher total age when time runs out wins). A round lasts about three to nine minutes.
    • "Alley Boss Challenge": a ladder across six neighborhoods, each with six named crews with different habits (36 rounds in all), opened one after another by beating the previous crew, plus free play with settings of your choice
    • A choice of three control styles (floating joystick / drag to steer / tap to go)
  2. The Game is free to download, and there is no limit on the number of rounds you can play in a day. When you lose a crew challenge, you can watch a reward ad to the end to retry with everyone's ages kept; in a timed round, when the bell rings and your team's total is a little behind, you can watch a reward ad to the end to play Overtime (each once per round). The number and conditions of Retry and Overtime and the ad conditions in Article 7 may change under Article 5.
  3. The Game can be played offline without a network connection. An internet connection is required only for displaying ads; without one, you cannot use Retry or Overtime by watching a reward ad.
